How it works — v37

Core rule: Basic = MAX(MW, Wages × 50%). PF, ESIC, Bonus, Gratuity, HRA auto-calculated.

Reverse mode: Target CTC or Net Take Home → engine back-solves Total Wages in 3 passes.

Formula Builder: Reference columns with [KEY]. Functions: IF, MIN, MAX, ROUND, ABS, SUM, AVG, AND, OR, NOT, SQRT.
